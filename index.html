How it works

How the money moves

Simple and transparent — the customer pays once, the platform takes its fee, the chef gets the rest.

Try it: move the order total

Chef receives$36.00
Platform commission$4.00

The commission is the platform's business income. Chefs receive their payout (with a payment record for their own taxes) — each side reports its own income.

The flow, end to end

  1. Customer orders & pays for same-day pickup in the app.
  2. Chef accepts & cooks within their daily cap and pickup window.
  3. Customer picks up with an order code — door, lobby, or curbside.
  4. Money splits automatically: chef payout minus the platform fee, via Stripe Connect.
  5. Safety ladder: food-safety issues escalate fast (warn at 2, block at 3 in the pilot rules); service issues warn at 3, block at 5 — humans approve every block.

What other platforms charge

Commission taken from the seller's side of each order. Delivery apps charge the most because they run a driver fleet — and their customers usually pay delivery and service fees on top.

hometable — pickup only10%

Why 10% fits this platform

  • ✓No delivery fleet to pay — customers pick up themselves, so the biggest DoorDash cost never exists here.
  • ✓Home chefs run thin margins — a lower fee keeps small MEHKO kitchens listing instead of cooking only for themselves.
  • ✓It still covers the platform's costs — card processing (about 2.9% + 30¢ per order via Stripe) comes out of the 10%, with room left for running the service.
  • ✓It undercuts DoorDash's lowest tier — an easy selling point when recruiting chefs: keep 90% of every order.
